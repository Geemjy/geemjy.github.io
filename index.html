Biography

Hello, I am Jooyeon GEEM (김주연). I am interested in small bodies in the Solar System, especially carbonaceous asteroids. My primary research techniques are observational methods, including polarimetry and spectroscopy. I also developed a polarimeter, SQUIDPOL, with Dr. Sunho Jin for observations of small bodies in the Solar System.

* Note: Jooyeon KIM is my passport name. I use GEEM for academic activity due to the overwhelming number of people with the last name KIM.
